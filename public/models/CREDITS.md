## robot-meshy7.glb

- Generated by **Meshy-7** (commercial text/image-to-3D API), prompt: "a robot".
- Source: [3D Arena dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/3d-arena/3d-arena) — `outputs/Meshy-7/a_robot.glb`, license **MIT**.
- Modified for the web with `gltf-transform optimize`: simplified from 1,944,440 to 58,326 triangles, texture 2048→1024 WebP, meshopt compression (76 MB → 643 KB).
- Shown on the landing page as an example of a specialist's output. It was **not** generated through Rialto.
